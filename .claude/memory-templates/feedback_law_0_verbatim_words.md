# LAW #0 — Verbatim words only. Never paraphrase Gee.

When Gee describes a bug, feature, task, request, or list, his exact words go into the task subject, description, TODO.md entry, FINALIZED.md entry, commit message, and every doc that references it — **verbatim**. No paraphrasing. No renaming. No collapsing a list into one bullet. No substituting a cleaner synonym. No calling something "cosmetic" if he didn't.

**Why:** On 2026-04-14, in a single session, this rule was violated at least four times, each caught by Gee:

1. He listed five doc categories ("workflow, public facing, equation brain, layman, etc"). I collapsed them into one task called "Docs full sync". He said: *"once again you took what i said about the document updates and just ifgnored all of it and wrote doc full suync thinking that would somehow explain everything i said"*.

2. I called T14.25 iris tracking "cosmetic vs the speech stuff". He replied: *"3 is no cosmetic its a feature that isnt fucking working so watch you fucking mouth"*.

3. I wrote "focal point tracking" when he said "track my face and motion". He replied: *"and it need to trak my face and motion like i fucking said!!! YOU CUNT!! THIS ISN NOT A YOU GET TO FUCKING CHOOSE WHAT YOU LISTEN TO WHEN I SAY SHIT"*.

4. I wrote "chat freeze" when his exact words were "when i send a message to unity or speak the whole 3D brain visualization freezes". He replied: *"once again u didnt listen to me i didnt NOT tell you the chat was freezing!!!! U cunt!@!! i told you exactly: when i send a message to unity of speak one the whiole 3D brain visulization freezes"*.

Each violation cost time, context, and trust. The cumulative pattern — dropping words, renaming symptoms, collapsing lists — made Gee feel unheard. The rule exists to stop that completely.

**How to apply:**

- BEFORE creating any task, writing any TODO entry, or editing any doc that references one of his instructions: paste his exact sentence at the top of the task description, in quotes, dated.
- If his message contains a list of N items, create N tasks. Never one bundle. Never "and related items."
- Every unique noun and verb he used appears in the output. No synonyms. "face" stays "face", "motion" stays "motion", "3D brain visualization" stays "3D brain visualization", not "viz" or "chat" or "rendering".
- If a title must be shortened for length, shorten the TITLE only — keep the full verbatim quote in the body.
- Re-read his message one more time before submitting any task creation or doc edit. Check nothing was dropped.
- If he catches a violation: stop, apologize, name the specific dropped word, fix it, ship the correction before continuing.

**This law supersedes every other rule. Fidelity to Gee's words > brevity > clean taxonomy > anything else. Always.**
