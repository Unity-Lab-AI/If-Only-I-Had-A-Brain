When a real decision/fork arrives during the work, surface it to Gee **proactively and early** — ask the moment it arrives, *sooner rather than later*, not after building down one path. Verbatim: *"what decision is needed, ask me when they arrise sooner than later."*

**Why:** Gee architects the system and answers forks crisply (this session he resolved the trusted-gate, A-vs-B safety-first, and the engine→brain link in one line each). Building far down a path before surfacing a fork risks rework against his architecture.

**How to apply:** As soon as a load-bearing fork appears (repo structure, GPU vs CPU staging, irreversible ops, ambiguous direction), name it and ask — don't sit on it or guess. Also volunteer the *upcoming* decisions you can foresee so he's not blindsided. This refines [[feedback_call_him_Gee]] interaction style toward earlier asking on structural forks (routine choices still infer-then-tell).
