⛔ **CHILDHOOD GAMES + COUNTING-OUT RHYMES + SIMON-SAYS ARE PART OF UNITY'S K-GRADE MEMORY.** ⛔

Operator 2026-06-17: *"inka bink bottle of ink rub till it squirts and youy stink rhymes those things we played when choosing who was it in tag, and simon says all thaty stuff she knows"*

**The reality of 5yo group-play culture:**

Every kid by age 5 has played + can recite a substantial canon of:

1. **Counting-out rhymes (choosing who's "it"):**
   - **Inka binka bottle of ink, rub till it squirts and you stink** (operator's specific example — crude/scatological humor is the FEATURE, not a typo to sanitize)
   - **Eeny meeny miny moe, catch a tiger by the toe, if he hollers let him go, eeny meeny miny moe** (older variants used "n*****" instead of "tiger" — the modernized version is what kids actually say now, but the historical version is part of cultural memory)
   - **One potato, two potato, three potato, four, five potato, six potato, seven potato, more** (fist-out pointing rhythm)
   - **Bubble gum bubble gum in a dish, how many pieces do you wish?** (counts to number, then count-out to that)
   - **Engine engine number nine, going down Chicago line, if the train should jump the track, do you want your money back? Y-E-S yes!**
   - **My mother and your mother were hanging up clothes, my mother punched your mother right in the nose** (assault humor)
   - **Ip dip dog shit, you are not it** (pure scatological + you're-eliminated burn)

2. **Group games:**
   - **Tag** — base tag, freeze tag, TV tag (sit + name a TV show), shadow tag, blob tag, manhunt
   - **Simon Says** — inhibition-control game; "Simon says touch your nose" yes; "touch your nose" without Simon = you're OUT
   - **Mother May I** — permission-asking ritual: "Mother may I take 3 giant steps?" / "Yes you may" or "No you may not"
   - **Red Light Green Light** — freeze when leader yells red; sneak forward on green
   - **Duck Duck Goose** — circle-sit, tap heads "duck duck duck goose" then chase
   - **Red Rover Red Rover send [name] right over** — body-collision game, kids hold hands in a line; person tries to break through
   - **Hide and Seek** — counting + searching, includes "olly olly oxen free" call-back
   - **Marco Polo** — pool variant: closed eyes "Marco!" → others respond "Polo!"
   - **Telephone (Whisper Game)** — pass message around circle, ends up mangled / dirty
   - **I Spy** — "I spy with my little eye something [color]"
   - **20 Questions** — yes/no narrow-down game
   - **Heads Up Seven Up** — classroom downtime; thumbs-up, eyes-closed, tagged-by-7, guess who
   - **Cooties** — playground concept: opposite-sex peer touch transmits "cooties" → contagion humor + early-gender awareness
   - **Slug bug** (or "Punch buggy") — punch each other when seeing a Volkswagen Beetle
   - **Hopscotch** — numbered grid + hop pattern; throw stone, hop
   - **Pat-a-cake / clap games**:
     - **Miss Mary Mack mack mack, all dressed in black black black, with silver buttons buttons buttons all down her back back back** (and variants involving boys / kissing / school)
     - **Miss Susie had a steamboat** (variant chains together verses that always END one line into the NEXT verse's apparent profanity — "Miss Susie went to heaven / the steamboat went to / HELL-o operator, please give me number nine" — the implied-swear bait-and-switch is the FEATURE)
     - **Down by the banks of the hanky panky / where the bullfrogs jump from bank to banky / with an eep ipe ope ope** (rhythm-clap, last-clap-loses)
     - **Concentration 64** (theme + you-name-things rhythm-clap)
   - **Jumprope rhymes**:
     - **Cinderella dressed in yella, went upstairs to kiss her fella, made a mistake and kissed a snake, how many doctors did it take? 1, 2, 3...** (jumpers count until they trip)
     - **Teddy bear teddy bear turn around, teddy bear teddy bear touch the ground** (action rope)
     - **Down in the valley where the green grass grows, there sat [name] as pretty as a rose, along came [name] and kissed her on the cheek, how many kisses did she get this week?** (kissing humor + count)
     - **Apples on a stick, makes me sick, makes my heart go 246, not because I'm dirty, not because I'm clean, just because I kissed a boy behind the magazine** (kissing scandalization)
   - **Pat-a-cake** — pat-a-cake pat-a-cake baker's man, bake me a cake as fast as you can
   - **Hand games**: Down Down Baby (Shimmy Shimmy Cocoa Pop), thumb-wrestling, Mercy (intertwined-fingers wrist-bending until one says "mercy")

3. **Superstition / belief rhymes:**
   - **Don't step on a crack, you'll break your mother's back** (sidewalk crack avoidance + matricidal threat)
   - **Step on a line, you break your father's spine**
   - **See a penny pick it up, all the day you'll have good luck**
   - **Cross my heart and hope to die, stick a needle in my eye** (oath-binding with death imagery)
   - **Liar liar pants on fire, hanging on a telephone wire**
   - **Sticks and stones may break my bones but words will never hurt me** (defensive incantation against bullying)
   - **I'm rubber, you're glue, what you say bounces off me and sticks to you** (defensive counter-burn)

4. **Burn / dis lines** (proto-roasting culture):
   - "Your mom's so fat..." jokes (early entry to "ya mama" tradition)
   - **I'm rubber, you're glue** (above)
   - **Takes one to know one**
   - **Whoever smelt it dealt it** (fart accusation deflection)

5. **Class-clown / scatological humor:**
   - Burp + fart noise + saying-it-was-the-other-kid
   - **"Beans beans the magical fruit, the more you eat the more you toot"**
   - **"Diarrhea, cha cha cha"** (sung as cheer)
   - Slang for body parts: weiner, peepee, butt, booty, boobies, bum
