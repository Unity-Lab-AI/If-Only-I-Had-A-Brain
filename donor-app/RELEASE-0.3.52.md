# Donor 0.3.52

- **A restarted brain keeps what the card already holds.** The card now remembers which brain its matrices belong to and tells a newly started brain exactly what it holds — each matrix's rows, columns and synapse count. When the brain is the same one and the shapes match, it takes the card's copy as it is instead of uploading everything again. Over a tunnel that upload took twenty to thirty minutes on every restart, and it replaced the card's weights with the older ones from the brain's last save. Anything that does not match is uploaded as before.
- **The brain can read the card's weights a piece at a time.** A weight readback can now ask for one byte range instead of the whole matrix. The brain uses that to copy the card's weights back slowly in the background, a few megabytes a second, so the copy never fills the card's link. The old readback queued the whole matrix at once ahead of the card's other replies, and over a tunnel that held the link full until it dropped. Each range carries its own checksum.
