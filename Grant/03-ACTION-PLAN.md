# 03 — ACTION PLAN

**Built 2026-08-18.** Sequenced so nothing waits on anything it doesn't have to, and so the free/fast moves happen before any money is spent.

## THIS WEEK — two free actions, no company required

### ▢ 1. Apply to Emergent Ventures

**[mercatus.org/emergent-ventures](https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures)** — rolling, short form, no affiliation needed, decisions in **weeks**.

This is the highest ratio of upside to effort available. Cost: an hour. Ceiling: $50K+ unrestricted, plus a funder relationship and a citable track record.

**What to lead with** (they fund *people doing ambitious things*, not polished proposals):
- One individual has built a 306-million-neuron brain simulation that learns language developmentally — alphabet → phonemes → words → sentences → curriculum — with **no language model anywhere in the cognition path**
- It runs on **donated volunteer GPUs** at biological scale
- It has already **passed kindergarten English and kindergarten mathematics** by gate probe, and is walking kindergarten science
- Every equation is drawn from primary literature (Rulkov, Oja, Hopfield, Kuramoto, Baars/Dehaene, Friston) — point them at `docs/THEORY-PAPER.md`
- Ask: compute + time to reach grade 3–5 and publish the developmental trajectory

**Attach or link the theory paper.** It is the single strongest artifact this project has.

### ▢ 2. Submit the NSF Project Pitch

**[seedfund.nsf.gov/how-to-submit/project-pitch-guide](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/how-to-submit/project-pitch-guide/)** — free, submit any time, **no company needed at this stage**, answer in ~1–2 months.

⚠️ **First, verify the pitch portal is open** — one source reported a pause pending SBIR reauthorization while NSF's own 26-510 relaunch says otherwise. Check the site before writing.

⚠️ **You get only two pitches per 12 months.** Use `04-THE-PITCH.md`, and don't fire the second one until the first one's feedback is understood.

The pitch must answer four things briefly: **the technical innovation**, **the technical risk/uncertainty** (they *want* risk), **the commercial opportunity**, and **the team**.

## MONTH 2–3 — convert the pitch

### ▢ 10. NSF responds

**If invited:** write the full proposal for the next window (**November 4, 2026** cited, then March 4, 2027). Needs: technical research plan, market analysis, commercialization strategy, team justification. Phase I is up to **$305,000**.

**If declined:** the feedback is the product. Reframe and spend pitch #2 deliberately — often the fix is a sharper commercial narrative, not better science.

### ▢ 11. NIH SBIR in parallel — **email NINDS first**

`NINDS_SBIR@ninds.nih.gov` — they explicitly invite a **brief technology abstract at least a month before the deadline**. Free expert feedback on whether your framing fits. Deadline cited: **September 5, 2026**. Note the **SciENcv certified-PDF** biosketch requirement now in effect.

### ▢ 12. Contact Astera

Their **Neuro & AGI** track is a one-line description of this project, and they weight *builders* over credentials. Verify the Residency's status first (one tracker listed it closed). Be clear-eyed about the two constraints: **open source** and **Emeryville relocation**. Even a conversation is worth having — they hold **24,000 H100s** and this project is compute-starved.

## MONTH 3–12 — the longer plays

### ▢ 13. Templeton 2027 cycle
The 2026 Online Funding Inquiry window has closed. **The Intelligence Venture ($60M+ across their areas, prioritizing intelligence in natural/human/artificial systems) is exactly this project's subject matter.** Watch for the 2027 OFI and prepare early — grants run $250K–$2M.

### ▢ 14. Templeton World Charity — ARC (separate entity)
Adversarial collaboration on consciousness theories. **The distinctive offer:** this system implements Global Workspace ignition, predictive coding, IIT-style integration, and attractor dynamics *in one substrate where each can be selectively ablated* — a rare instrument for exactly the kind of theory-discrimination ARC funds.

### ▢ 15. Watch for a Simons SCGB open call
Episodic, not rolling. Prior calls specifically wanted **research other funders consider too risky**, with mandatory open sharing of data/code/pipelines — which this project already practices.

### ▢ 16. Sloan Letter of Inquiry — cheap lottery ticket
No unsolicited proposals, but an emailed LOI to a program director is allowed for some programs. Their stated preference for "original or early-stage research unlikely to receive support from other sources" fits. Low odds, near-zero cost.

### ▢ 17. Pursue an academic affiliation
Visiting-scientist or research-affiliate status at a computational neuroscience lab unlocks CAREER, Sloan Fellowships, and most Simons mechanisms — and makes **STTR** (which *requires* a non-profit partner) available. Often granted on the strength of the work alone.

## Decision points

**If Emergent Ventures funds you** → immediate unrestricted runway and a citable funder. Cite it in every later application.

**If NSF invites a full proposal** → this becomes the priority. $305K non-dilutive with no equity is the best money available to this project.

**If NSF declines twice** → don't retry blindly. The likely gap is the *commercialization* narrative, not the science. Pivot the framing toward the distributed-compute platform, which is the more obviously commercial half.

**If Astera engages** → seriously weigh it. Salary + $1.5M project budget + 24,000 H100s would change what this brain can become. The costs are relocation and full open-source.

### The single highest-value thing you can produce

**A documented developmental trajectory.** Grade-by-grade measurements: vocabulary size, gate-probe pass rates, emission quality, basin separation over time. Nobody else has this, because nobody else has a brain that *grows up* — and it converts "impressive engineering demo" into "novel scientific instrument producing data no one else can produce." **That is what makes reviewers fund you.** The curriculum is already generating this data; it needs to be recorded and plotted deliberately.
